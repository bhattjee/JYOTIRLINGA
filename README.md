## 🙏 About This Project

**Jyotirling** is a comprehensive digital platform dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich cultural and spiritual heritage of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas - the most revered temples of Lord Shiva across India. This repository serves as a detailed guide for devotees, pilgrims, and cultural enthusiasts seeking authentic information about these divine destinations.

![Traditional Border](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fb475bd-6168-489f-8574-9a649bb4b6cd)

## ✨ Features

- **Complete Jyotirling Guide**: Detailed information about all 12 sacred Jyotirlingas
- **High-Quality Images**: Authentic photographs and artwork of each temple
- **Historical Context**: Rich backstories and mythological significance
- **Pilgrimage Information**: Travel guides, timings, and visitor information
- **Cultural Heritage**: Traditional stories, legends, and spiritual practices
- **Interactive Experience**: User-friendly navigation and engaging content
- **Mobile Responsive**: Accessible across all devices for devotees worldwide

## 🏛️ The 12 Sacred Jyotirlingas

### 1. **Somnath Temple** - Gujarat
*The Eternal Shrine*

<div align="center">

![Somnath Temple](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/15d35cd0-7ae9-4cce-948e-1f5df3bcb496)

**Location**: Prabhas Patan, Gujarat  
**Significance**: The first among the 12 Jyotirlingas, known as the "Eternal Shrine"  
**Legend**: Where the Moon God was cursed and later blessed by Lord Shiva

*The Sacred Hill*

**Significance**: Located on the holy Srisailam hills

*The Lord of Time*
